\field{The ask}
Orchestrate \bead{16s}, \bead{ula} and \bead{cue} as one batch. All three are the
same shape --- a helper or a parameter that no longer earns its place --- and none buys any
correctness, so the binding constraint was that each must move \emph{no observable}: not a gate
count, not a golden byte, not a ctest case name. A refactor in this batch that changed a
measured number would be a failed refactor, not a discovered bug.


\field{What landed}
Three consolidations, each written in an isolated worktree, reviewed
against three questions, given one fix round, and merged serially with a gate after each.

\bead{16s} removed \texttt{dr\_sweep}'s three \texttt{(void)}-cast parameters and the two enum
constants they orphaned. \bead{ula} replaced three copies of a fork-and-capture helper with
\texttt{tests/support/childcap.[ch]}, carrying forward the six requirements the bead had written
down so they would not be re-derived. \bead{cue} moved six private copies of \texttt{all\_const}
into \texttt{cq\_bits\_all\_const} in \texttt{src/kernels/kernel.h} and seventeen copies of the
death-test null sink into \texttt{cq\_death\_null\_sink} in \texttt{tests/support/death.h}.

Three things the review round established that the implementing agents had not. First, the
\texttt{L}-not-\texttt{W} miscompile that \bead{cue} was built around \emph{is} detected: mutating
\texttt{shift\_var.c}'s call to pass \texttt{W} reddens \texttt{test\_kernel\_shift\_var} in both
configurations, and under that mutant the \emph{value} check passes while only the pool assertion
fails. Second, \bead{ula}'s own argument that its \texttt{SIGABRT} test was unfalsifiable rested on
an incomplete enumeration of child outcomes, and a paired mutation located the work on exactly that
line. Third, a dated re-measurement note in \bead{16s} was itself wrong, and the orchestrator had
re-typed it into an agent brief as measured fact.

One defect was introduced and caught: \texttt{cq\_death\_null\_sink} declared its vtable on
automatic storage where the seventeen file-statics it replaced were zero-initialised static storage,
converting M04's loud by-name NULL-entry guard into a jump through stack garbage should a seventh
entry ever be added. Corrected to \texttt{= \{0\}}.

\field{Not taken}
\textbf{The argument \bead{16s} rejected}, recorded because it is the
better half of the case and the bead stated it correctly: unlike a literal \texttt{1} meaning
``exhaustive'', each removed argument was a real measurement a reader could use. It lost because
nothing printed those numbers at those widths and \texttt{dr\_sweep} discarded all three, while both
measurements survive at the \texttt{i128} cases that do print them --- and because the budget
constant read at the call site as a cap on a sweep that has had no cap since 2026-08-20.

\textbf{A date discrepancy was investigated and declined}, not fixed: the two dates the reviewer
read as contradictory are two different events, and \texttt{test\_kernel\_mux.c} states the split
outright, so there was nothing to correct.

\textbf{Nine death files were deliberately not converted} --- a leak-detecting sink whose entries
disarm the death window, an \texttt{nmz} tripwire backing a \texttt{FAIL\_REGULAR\_EXPRESSION},
four counting sinks whose cases read the count, and two where the sink is the subject under test.
Converting any of them would be a silent loss of what that file measures.

\textbf{The remedy \bead{09z} originally prescribed was withdrawn before anyone built it.} A
negative control that prints and \emph{returns} kills only one of the two mutants it was meant to
kill, because a returning child yields status 0 and the two spellings agree; the discriminating set
is two controls, one that \texttt{\_exit(1)}s and one that raises a non-\textsc{sigabrt} signal.

\textbf{\texttt{CQOPS\_UPDATE\_GOLDENS} was forbidden for the batch} in every configuration, for
every agent and reviewer, and was never set. It is the documented way to make a red L4 green, so
for a change that must move no observable it can only launder a failure.
