\field{The ask}
A design question, not a bead: could libcqops shrink to the seven gate primitives if CQ\_lang
lowered arithmetic to \texttt{i1} gates itself --- one \texttt{cq\_theta\_i32} becoming 32
\texttt{cq\_theta\_i1}, every adder a sea of \texttt{and}/\texttt{xor} --- and would that inherit
clang's optimisations? Argued first, then, at the user's request, \emph{measured} with a one-opcode
prototype: the same K6 ripple carry in two spellings, applied four times so clang has a chain to
work on, at W=8 and then W=32.

\field{What landed}
\texttt{tools/bitlevel/} --- \texttt{gen\_fixture.py} emits one CQ program in two spellings at
width W (\texttt{-DBITLEVEL=0}: unsigned \texttt{+}, lowered by the pass to
\texttt{cq\_template\_add}; \texttt{=1}: the identical construction written over tainted
\texttt{bool}s, which is exactly what a bit-level lowering would hand the pass), with
\texttt{-DCHAIN} choosing four distinct addends or the same one four times;
\texttt{bitlevel.py} runs both through CQ\_lang's real front end and pass, links against this
archive with a pool probe on the line, runs quantum and classical, and tabulates gates, peak,
strands, ABI calls, stage timings and lowered-IR size. Neither repository needed a change to run
the experiment, and the decision it produced is \textbf{PRD~\S15 D27}.

\field{Measured}
Release archive, CQ\_lang \texttt{62b0b111}, printf sink, \texttt{clang -O1} (\texttt{-O2}
identical; \texttt{-O0} declines in the pass for both spellings), four-deep add chain.

\field{Not taken}
The bit-level backend itself, on the measurement. Three things the row records so they are not
re-derived: the Toffoli halving is global-versus-local \emph{uncompute} (kernel sandwich
$\times$ pass \texttt{\_unc}), not bit granularity, and is reachable at the opcode boundary by a
kernel that hands its scratch to the pass (derived, not measured, and a CQ\_lang ABI addition);
half the $2\times$ peak is SSA out-of-place mapping the pass could remove with three rewrites,
the other half \emph{is} the Toffoli saving --- Bennett's pebble game; and clang's integer algebra
lands on the opcode spelling only, so bits must be expanded \emph{after} value-level clang, and
even then clang deletes an expanded uncompute rather than optimising it. Also not taken: a
second clang run over the lowered IR (measured inert), and the hand-written W=8 fixture, replaced
by the generator once the generator reproduced its numbers.
