\field{The ask}
Restore the floating-point tests to a small representative constant number of
circuit executions, retain broad semantic coverage on classical paths, and
estimate the time saved without running the tests.

\field{What landed}
Every floating-point L1 shape now selects at most eight representative anchors
from its complete semantic table.  The driver again uses one shared 32-case
budget with no per-shape floor: eight anchors at three mask rows, the two
mandatory mask cases, and six deterministic random cases.  Full NaN, infinity,
zero, subnormal, rounding, overflow, saturation, and kernel-specific tables
remain checked by classical/reference cases.  The redundant full-table circuit
loops in 	exttt{fcmp} and 	exttt{fpfield} were removed; the class-block loop
also uses the same representative subset.  PRD-v2~\S7.12 and K17--K19 record
the split between complete classical tables and bounded circuit samples.
