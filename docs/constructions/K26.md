**Bennett source:** `third_party/bennett/src/extract/instructions.jl` lines 4826–4946
(pinned commit 980805de85314b3da7ac25cf6454b56566f8e609) — the extractor's intrinsic

## 2. The construction

### 2.0 Decisions the transcription rests on

- **D-K26-1 — one row per IR instruction, literal (PRD-v2 §7.2).** Every IRBinOp `add`/`or`,
  IRICmp and IRSelect is one row, W lanes wide (every instruction carries width `w`), no CSE, no
  narrowing. The closing `IRBinOp(dest, :add, ssa(prev), iconst(0), w)` of every arm is a real
  `add` row — upstream's comment calls it a rename; the IR is an operator occurrence. `ctlz`/
  `cttz`'s seed `add(iconst(w), iconst(0))` is a real row although both operands are constants:
  the §3 fold table elides its constant-controlled gates, which is §7.3's shape.
- **D-K26-2 — `icmp ne` is M16's `eq` block read through its RAW flag.** `lower_icmp!`'s `:ne` row
  is `lower_not1!(lower_eq!(…))` (arith.jl:410), and `lower_eq!` itself ends `CNOT(or, r);
  NOT(r)` (:445). M16's `cq_eq_block` exports the flag *before* that trailing pair (`a != b`,
  cmp.h), so the two NOTs cancel and `ne` IS the raw flag — the same reading M31's class block
  takes for `fadd.jl`'s `!=` and K09's `cq_kernel_ne` for its copy-out. No `not1` row.
- **D-K26-3 — constant `lshr` / `shl` / `and` are VIEWS (PRD-v2 §7.3 as amended).** A view is
  `((base >> shr) & low(keep)) << shl` over the rail, a constant or an earlier row's output,
  with **Bennett's constant-shift semantics**: a shift by `W` empties the vector (arith.jl:305–320).
  No shift in these five arms reaches `W` (the largest is `W - 1`), so D8 never applies.
- **D-K26-4 — `ctlz` / `cttz` take no `is_zero_poison` operand.** The arms read only `ops[1]`
  (:4852–4853, :4874–4875) and seed the answer with `iconst(w)`, so `x == 0` gives `W`
  whatever the flag says: deterministic, D3's posture, and exactly LLVM's non-poison value. The
  shim (a later bead) drops the i1 immarg.
- **D-K26-5 — the R9 short-circuit is a bit-serial statement of the intrinsic, not a
  transcription of the expansion.** Integer semantics are host-independent (PRD-v2 §7.4's reason
  for transcribing does not arise), and add.c's `fold_constant` is the precedent. Width-generic
  over the lanes, never a packed scalar (I5). L1 checks it against an independent oracle on every
  all-classical case.
- **D-K26-6 — `bswap` at `W % 16 != 0` is a hard error** in both configurations (LLVM's verifier
  rejects it; `w ÷ 8` at :4926 would silently drop a byte at W = 24). ctpop/ctlz/cttz/bitreverse
  are defined at every `W` in `[1, 128]`.
- **D-K26-7 — the scratch is FLAT and laid out by one prefix walk** (K12's D9 shape, M36's
  template): row `i`'s span starts at `off + Σ_{j<i} region(row j)`, `off` added in one place.

### 2.3 I6

Every target is a bit of its row's own span; the rail, its views and the constant spans reach
the emitter only through `const cq_bit *` controls. `dst` is written only by the copy-out.

## 3. Gate counts — MEASURED (Release, all-quantum rail, values all-ones), and closed forms

Forward and `_unc` are identical at this mask for all five (asserted per W by
`tests/goldens/ibits.counts`, not as an invariant — Rule 14).

| Intrinsic | NOT | CNOT | Toffoli | Valid for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ctpop` | 0 | 4W² + W | 2W² − 2 | W ≥ 1 |
| `ctlz` / `cttz` | 2·S(W) | 8W² + 5W | 4W² + 2W − 4 | W ≥ 1 |
| `bitreverse` | 0 | 2W² + 3W | 4W − 6 (0 at W = 1) | W ≥ 1 |
| `bswap` | 0 | W²/4 + 3W | 4W − 34 | W ≡ 0 mod 16 |

`S(W) = Σ_{v=0}^{W} popcount(v)` — the NOTs are the set lanes of the constant seed `W` and of the
`W` select arms `iconst(0 … W−1)`, each demoted from a CNOT by a `ONE` control.

Derivation (compute half, then `2·compute + W` CNOT copy-out): a one-hot view (`bit`) folds
every lane but one; `add … iconst(0)` folds its `b` CNOTs and every `a·b` Toffoli; `ctlz`'s `ne`
is `1 + 2(W−1)` CNOT and `W−1` Toffoli. Measured rows (forward):

| W | ctpop | ctlz = cttz | bitreverse | bswap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | (0, 5, 0) | (2, 13, 2) | (0, 5, 0) | — |
| 8 | (0, 264, 126) | (26, 552, 268) | (0, 152, 26) | — |

## 5. Deltas from upstream

- Rule 2's local sandwich (×2 + W copy-out) where upstream relies on its global reverse.
- Constants are sources and constant shifts/ands are views, so our region is below upstream's
  wire count for the same expansion (PRD-v2 §7.3).
- `ne` reads M16's raw flag (D-K26-2): two NOTs fewer than a gate-for-gate `lower_not1!(lower_eq!)`.

## 6. `fshl` / `fshr` — NOT LANDED: the expansion is not LLVM's funnel shift

LLVM defines `fshl(a, b, s)` / `fshr(a, b, s)` with `s` taken **modulo W**, returning `a` (fshl) /
`b` (fshr) at `s ≡ 0`. The expansion is `(a << s) | (b >> (w − s))` (fshl, :4955–4969) and
`(a << (w − s)) | (b >> s)` (fshr, :4980–4994), with a `ConstOperand` branch that folds `w − s`.

**The branch rule the kernel would use** (recorded as decided by the brief): an ALL-CLASSICAL
shift operand takes the constant branch (constant shifts are views), anything else the variable
branch (M12's barrel for the shifts, M14's `sub` for `w − s`).

**Neither branch equals LLVM on every shift value.** Measured 2026-09-24 by a scratch program
(not in the repo) at W ∈ {8, 16, 32, 64}, s ∈ [0, 2W+1], both intrinsics, 496 cases:

- **Variable branch, run through our real v1 kernels** (`cq_kernel_shl_var`/`lshr_var`,
  `cq_kernel_sub`, `cq_kernel_or`, all-quantum): wrong at every `s ≡ 0 mod W` — the `w − s`
  amount is `W` (mod 2^W), which the barrel reads mod 2^⌈log₂W⌉ as a shift by **0**, so the
  "empty" half is the whole other operand: `a | b`. This is Bennett's own barrel semantics
  (arith.jl:333–348, "the shift amount is taken mod (next power of two ≥ W)") and D8's, not a
  divergence of ours. **Minimal witness: W = 8, fshl(a = 0x00, b = 0x01, s = 0) → port 0x01,
  LLVM 0x00; fshr(a = 0x01, b = 0x00, s = 0) → port 0x01, LLVM 0x00.**
- **Constant branch, under Bennett's constant-shift semantics** (a model, since it is views):
  right at `s ∈ [0, W−1]` (s = 0 works: `b >> W` is empty), **wrong at `s = W`** (`a << W`
  empty, `b >> 0` = b: fshl(0x01, 0x00, 8) → port 0x00, LLVM 0x01), and **refused at `s > W`**
  — `_check_const_shift` (arith.jl:301–303) throws on `iconst(w − s) < 0`, so upstream does not
  compile a shift LLVM defines.

Both rotations (`a == b`) come out right at `s ≡ 0` by accident (`a | a = a`); the ABI's class-2
funnel rows (`intrinsic_table.yaml:129-130`, `fwd_qql`) do not have `a == b`. Rule 1 forbids
inventing the `s mod W` / `s == 0` guard, so fshl/fshr stay unlanded and the defect is owed
upstream by the maintainer (9ve.34's shape).

## 8. Confidence

High for the five landed intrinsics: every row cites its push line; values are checked against
an independent oracle (compiler builtins plus loops) at W ∈ {1,2,3,4,5,8,16,32,64} (bswap
{16,32,48,64}); the slot scan re-derives every operand lane from the Julia rather than from the
row descriptors.

### 8.1 Provocation (2026-09-24, a scratch battery; Release for the suite, both configs for deaths)

30 mutants, 29 killed. The three "optimisation" mutants — dropping ctpop's closing `add 0`,
ctlz's seed `add` as an `or`, bitreverse's `or` as an `add` — keep every VALUE and are killed
only by `each_program_is_the_sum_of_its_blocks` (and a golden); dropping the step machine's `off`
only by `two_programs_in_one_region_do_not_collide`; `lower_or!` with its Toffoli first only by
the slot scan. Every guard deleted in `ibits*.c` reddened its own death case in both
configurations; the width door needed `width is not positive` banned, because kernel.h's
`cq_kernel_check_n` otherwise answered a W = 0 call for it.

**One survivor, an equivalent class:** reading the `ne` flag as `diff[0]` instead of
`cq_eq_flag`. Every `ne` here compares a ONE-HOT view against 0, so `diff[1..W-1]` are
provably 0 and the OR-prefix's last wire equals `diff[0]` in value; only the select's control
QUBIT differs, which no instrument in the repo checks (bd memories
a-slot-scan-cannot-see-which-operand-a-slot-reads). Not fixed: the accessor is M16's contract.
