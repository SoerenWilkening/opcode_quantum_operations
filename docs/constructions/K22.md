# K22 — IEEE binary64 field views and the four class predicates (`f64`)

**Module** M31 — `src/kernels/fpfield.[ch]` (views) and `src/kernels/fpclass.[ch]`
(predicates), split on PRD-v2 §5's recorded **VIEWS ↔ CLASS PREDICATES** seam.
**Number** assigned in PRD-v2 §5. **Bead** `opcode_quantum_operations-9ve.18`.
**Upstream** Bennett.jl at the pinned commit in `third_party/bennett/COMMIT`.

K22 is the first v2 kernel module. It ships no `cqrt_*` symbol of its own: its
consumers are M36 `fcmp` (which needs `a_nan`), M33 `fadd` (`a_nan`, `a_inf`,
`a_zero`), M35 `fdiv`, M37 `fconv` and M40 `fsqrt`, and each reaches it through
the exported **step block** rather than through a kernel, because `cq_sandwich`
refuses nesting in both configurations.

**Two things in this document are load-bearing and are easy to skim past.**
First, **slots and gates come apart here**, and M31 is the first module in the
project where they do — every composition identity below is over SLOTS and none
of them may be compared against a gate total. Second, the **views absorb both a
shift and a mask**, which is this module's one delta from a literal reading of
PRD-v2 §7.2 and is written out in full in §5.1 with the arithmetic that makes
the two readings agree lane for lane.

## 1. Julia source excerpt

Four predicates, three files, all at the pinned commit. Each is one line.

```julia
# fadd.jl:20-22 — the field unpack
    sa = a >> 63
    ea = (a >> 52) & UInt64(0x7FF)
    fa = a & FRAC_MASK

# fadd.jl:28-34 — "Special-case predicates (computed unconditionally)"
    a_nan  = (ea == UInt64(0x7FF)) & (fa != UInt64(0))
    b_nan  = (eb == UInt64(0x7FF)) & (fb != UInt64(0))
    a_inf  = (ea == UInt64(0x7FF)) & (fa == UInt64(0))
    b_inf  = (eb == UInt64(0x7FF)) & (fb == UInt64(0))
    a_zero = (ea == UInt64(0)) & (fa == UInt64(0))
    b_zero = (eb == UInt64(0)) & (fb == UInt64(0))

# flog.jl:262 — the subnormal test, spelled at f64
    a_subnormal = (ea == UInt64(0)) & (fa != UInt64(0))
```

`fcmp.jl:12-23` repeats the unpack and `a_nan`/`b_nan` verbatim, and
`fcmp.jl:117-125`'s `_either_nan` does it a third time; PRD-v2 §7.2 forbids
sharing them, so K18's `B_nan` will carry its own copy of this construction and
so will `fadd`'s. **`b_nan`/`b_inf`/`b_zero` are the same three predicates on
the second operand** — a consumer applies the block to `b` rather than asking
K22 for a second entry point.

**The subnormal row is `flog.jl:262` and NOT `fpconv.jl:54`.** The latter reads
`a_sub = (ea == UInt32(0)) & (fa != UInt32(0))` and is `f32`'s — a `UInt32`
intermediate with an 8-bit exponent field. Taking it would have been the right
shape at the wrong width, which is §7.7's wrong-`soft_*`-row trap in a new
column.

### 1.1 The constants

`softfloat_common.jl:8-14`, transcribed into `src/kernels/fpfield.h` in the

| name | value | what |
|---|---|---|
| `FRAC_MASK` | `0x000FFFFFFFFFFFFF` | the 52-bit stored fraction |

## 2. Gate sequence as indexed pseudo-code

### 2.0 What is transcribed, and at what grain

Three upstream primitives and nothing else.

| occurrence | upstream | libcqops | slots | scratch |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `x == y` / `x != y` | `lower_eq!`, `arith.jl:424-447` | **M16's `cq_eq_block`** through `cq_eq_step` | `cq_eq_steps(64)` | `2·64 − 1` |
| the `==` polarity | `lower_not1!`, `arith.jl:474-478` | emitted inline: `CX` then `X` | 2 | 1 |
| `&` | `lower_and!`, `arith.jl:268-272` | emitted inline: one `CCX` | 1 | 0 (targets the flag) |

`and`, `or` and `not1` are emitted directly by the step function, one gate per
slot, which is PRD-v2 §7.10's disposition — *"Bitwise ops are one gate per bit
and are emitted directly in the fp step function"* — and is what M19 already
does for its two CNOTs. They are **not** calls to `cq_kernel_and`: those are
whole Rule 7 kernels and one call would emit `n` gates in a single slot,
breaking the driver's one-involution-per-step contract.

**`!=` COSTS ONE BLOCK AND `==` COSTS TWO, AND THAT IS UPSTREAM'S ASYMMETRY.**
`cq_eq_flag`'s wire holds `a != b`: `lower_eq!` ends `CNOT(or[W-1], r); NOT(r)`
(`arith.jl:445`) and that trailing `NOT` is the COPY-OUT's, folded there by
K09.md §5 delta 2. So a `!=` occurrence reads the raw wire and an `==`
occurrence owes itself a `lower_not1!`. **Reading the flag the other way is
invisible to everything structural** — same gate count, same palindrome, clean
scratch — which is K9's own `uge`-meaning-`ule` one layer down.

### 2.1 The view wiring — zero gates, zero qubits

A **view** is an assembled `cq_bit[64]`: struct copies of the field's bits in
lanes `0..n)` and `CQ_BIT_ZERO` in lanes `n..64)`. It is read-only, it is only
ever a CONTROL, and it allocates nothing.

```
exp  = a[52..62] ++ ZERO[11..63]     /* (a >> 52) & 0x7FF   fadd.jl:21 */
frac = a[0..51]  ++ ZERO[52..63]     /* a & FRAC_MASK       fadd.jl:22 */
sign = a[63]     ++ ZERO[1..63]      /* a >> 63             fadd.jl:20 */
```

**ELEVEN LANES FOR THE EXPONENT, NOT TWELVE.** `a >> 52` is lanes 52..63 and
the `& 0x7FF` drops lane 63, the sign. A twelve-lane view computes
`ea == 0x7FF` correctly for every POSITIVE operand and wrongly for every
negative one, so it survives a golden, a palindrome and a positive-specials
anchor table. `tests/support/fpanchors.c`'s two negative singles — a negative
subnormal and −1.0 — are what separate the two readings at L1, and
`a_view_addresses_the_lanes_the_source_names` separates them index for index
one layer down.

The constant operands are `const cq_bit[64]` spans filled from a `uint64_t`
(PRD-v2 §7.3): `K_0x7FF` (lanes 0..10 ONE) and `K_ZERO`. Upstream allocates a
fresh wire per constant lane (`arith.jl:202`); we do not, and §7.3's
consequence is explicit — our peak is BELOW upstream's ancilla figure for the
same construction and the difference is not a bug to chase in either direction.

### 2.2 The compute half, block by block, in source order

One row per operator occurrence, as the source spells it. `C_eq = cq_eq_steps(64)`
is **asked of M16** and appears as a number nowhere in the kernel or in a test.

**`is_nan` — `(ea == 0x7FF) & (fa != 0)` (`fadd.jl:29`)**

| # | occurrence | block | operands | output | slots |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `ea == 0x7FF` | `eq`(64) | exp view, `K_0x7FF` | raw = `ea != 0x7FF` | `C_eq` |
| 2 | (polarity) | `not1` | raw | `ea_max` | 2 |
| 3 | `fa != 0` | `eq`(64) | frac view, `K_ZERO` | raw = `fa != 0` | `C_eq` |
| 4 | `&` | `and`(1) | `ea_max`, raw | **flag** | 1 |

**`is_inf` — `(ea == 0x7FF) & (fa == 0)` (`fadd.jl:31`)** — rows 1–3 as above,
then a second `not1` on row 3's raw wire, then the `and`. `2·C_eq + 5`.

**`is_zero` — `(ea == 0) & (fa == 0)` (`fadd.jl:33`)** — `is_inf`'s shape with
`K_ZERO` in place of `K_0x7FF`. `2·C_eq + 5`.

**`is_subnormal` — `(ea == 0) & (fa != 0)` (`flog.jl:262`)** — `is_nan`'s shape
with `K_ZERO` in place of `K_0x7FF`. `2·C_eq + 3`.

The four differ in **two constants and two polarity bits**, which is why
`src/kernels/fpclass.c` carries one table and one dispatch rather than four
copies — K9's own answer to the same question, and for K9's reason: four
hand-copied step dispatches are four independent chances at the one mistake
PRD-v2 §7.1 says a kernel of this shape actually makes.

### 2.3 The slot scan — written BEFORE the kernel

**The prediction is four-valued, and the stream has its own cursor.** At the
all-quantum mask every v1 kernel emits exactly one gate per slot, so K12's scan
could walk slot index and stream index together. That is false here: a control
lane that is a constant `ZERO` makes the gate vanish and a constant `ONE`
demotes it (`CCX→CX`, `CX→X`), and both come from the VIEWS and the CONSTANT
SPANS rather than from the operand's bit-kinds — so they happen at **every**
mask, including the one L4 pins. This is D-K18-6, written down for K18 and
needed here first.

```
sched_eq(n, v) = v < 2n ? CX : (((v - 2n) % 3 == 2) ? CCX : CX)   /* arith.jl:424-447 */
sched_not1(v)  = (v == 0) ? CX : X                                /* arith.jl:474-478 */
sched_and(v)   = CCX                                              /* arith.jl:268-272 */

predict(slot s):
    r    = the row containing s; v = s - base(r)     /* base from cq_eq_steps, ASKED */
    kind = sched_<r.block>(r.n, v)
    for each CONTROL lane c the gate reads:
        if c is a constant ZERO -> return NONE
        if c is a constant ONE  -> kind--            /* CCX->CX, CX->X */
    return kind
```

`CQ_OP_X`/`CX`/`CCX` are 0/1/2, so "demote" is a decrement; the test pins that
numbering with a `_Static_assert` rather than a comment, on `CQ_BIT_ZERO`'s
precedent.

**The scan's non-vacuity is asserted per class and EXACTLY.** `folded > 0`
holds for all four (a view fills 53 or 12 lanes with `CQ_BIT_ZERO` whatever the
mask). `demoted` does **not**: it is exactly `popcount(ce) + popcount(cf)`, so
`is_zero` and `is_subnormal` — both constants zero — legitimately demote
nothing, and a blanket `demoted > 0` is wrong. That was measured, not argued:
the first draft of the case asserted the inequality and went red on two of the
four classes. The popcount is also the sharper claim, and is what would notice a
constant span silently becoming all-zero.

**Phase B of an `eq` block never folds.** Its controls are `diff` and `orr`
bits, which the driver pre-materialises at step 0 under I6(b), so all `3(W−1)`
slots emit at every mask. Every fold in this module is in Phase A.

### 2.3a The span-disjointness scan — added after a measured survivor

**The slot scan above does NOT see the layout, and one mutant proves it.**
Collapsing both `eq` blocks onto offset 0 — so the fraction test overwrites the
exponent test's `diff` and `orr` — leaves the value right, the palindrome
intact, the pool restored, every gate count identical and the slot scan green.
Measured in both configurations, 2026-09-18, before this scan existed.

**And it is equivalent for a reason no assertion stated.** `lower_eq!` leaves
`diff` and `orr` all zero exactly when its own flag is 0, i.e. when the
operands were equal; and the `&` closing every one of the four rows masks the
second conjunct away in precisely the other case. So the second block sees a
clean region whenever its answer can matter. **That accident is one edit from
evaporating** — a row spelled `|` instead of `&` (which `soft_fcmp`'s
`either_nan` is, `fcmp.jl:124`), or a consumer reading the second block's raw
wire directly, and the same layout becomes a silent miscompile.

The instrument asserts the **layout**, not the accident: walking the same
slot→stream cursor, no qubit index may be written by two of the five spans
(`eq_e`, `ne_e`, `eq_f`, `ne_f`, flag). It reads no golden and is derived from
§2.2's table. **M33 and M36 will compose several of these blocks in one region
and inherit the same hazard**, so the scan is the thing to copy, not the
argument.

### 2.4 Scratch layout — flat, and every span named

One contiguous region, carved at `off`-relative offsets. `S_eq = 2·64 − 1 = 127`
is cmp.h's published contract for a consumer's layout.

| span | bits | present when |
|---|---:|---|
| `eq_e` internals (`diff` 64 ++ `orr` 63) | 127 | always |
| `ne_e` — `lower_not1!`'s fresh wire | 1 | the exponent occurrence is `==` (all four) |
| `eq_f` internals | 127 | always |
| `ne_f` | 1 | the fraction occurrence is `==` (`is_inf`, `is_zero`) |
| flag | 1 | always |

**EVERY SPAN IS `off`-RELATIVE, AND THAT IS THE ONE THING A SINGLE BLOCK CANNOT
CHECK.** Dropping `k->off` from the span helper slides the block wholesale
inside the caller's region: right value, palindrome intact, clean pool, every
case green. The only detector is **two blocks at two offsets in one region** —
they then occupy the same bits, the forward pair is still undone by the reverse
pair in exact reverse order so the pool and the palindrome survive, and the
**value** is the whole of the evidence.

### 2.5 The copy-out

**One CNOT, and no X.** The flag holds the predicate itself rather than its
negation, because each `==` occurrence's polarity was settled inside the block
by upstream's own `lower_not1!`. That is the opposite of K9, where the trailing
`NOT` folds into the copy-out — the difference is that K9's raw wire is the
final answer's complement and K22's is an intermediate that a later `and` reads.

### 2.6 I6(a) and I6(b)

**I6(a)** — every target a kernel names is a bit of the caller's region: an
`eq` block's `diff`/`orr`, a `not1`'s fresh wire, the flag. `a`, the views and
the constant spans are **only ever controls** — they reach the emitter through
`cq_emit_*`'s `const cq_bit *` parameters, so a source cannot be materialised
by construction. `dst` is touched exclusively in the copy-out, which the driver
runs with the extent deliberately disarmed.

**I6(b)** — the driver pre-materialises the whole region before any gate, so
every scratch bit is `CQ_BIT_Q` from step 0 and a step emits an identical gate
forwards and backwards. The consequence that differs from every v1 kernel:
pre-materialisation removes the *scratch* side's dependence on bit-kinds, but
**the views and the constant spans are not scratch** and keep their classical
lanes at every mask. §2.3 is how that is tested.

**A view is not an I2 violation.** A copied `cq_bit` with `kind == CQ_BIT_Q`
names the same qubit index as the bit it was copied from. I2 is about live
REGISTERS, and a view owns nothing, is never handed to `cq_reg_*` and is never
freed. What makes it sound is checkable rather than asserted: a view is only
ever a control, and Debug's `cq_bit_coincident` still fires if two controls of
one gate resolve to the same index.

## 3. Gate-count formula

### 3.1 The composition identity — over SLOTS

> `cq_fp_class_steps(cls) = 2 · cq_eq_steps(64) + n_eq · C_not1 + C_and1`

where `n_eq` is how many of the two occurrences the source spells `==`.
`cq_eq_steps(64)` is **asked of M16**; `C_not1 = 2` and `C_and1 = 1` are
transcribed from the two pinned upstream functions, as K18 §2.2 does for the
same inline primitives. The identity is asserted in
`each_predicate_is_two_eq_blocks_a_not1_per_eq_and_one_and`, which **reads no
golden**, so `CQOPS_UPDATE_GOLDENS=1` cannot bless a narrowing.

`C_eq(64)` is pinned separately, in a case that runs **no kernel**, so that a
sibling module's cost moving reddens that case and NAMES the block rather than
reddening the per-predicate identity with a number nobody can attribute.

### 3.2 Measured — Release, 2026-09-18, all-quantum on `a`

Slots, the compute half's emitted tuple, and the sandwiched kernel. `sandwiched
= 2 × compute + 1 CX`.

| predicate | slots | compute half (X, CX, CCX) | sandwiched (X, CX, CCX) | total |
|---|---:|---|---|---:|
| `fp_is_nan`       | 637 | (12, 316, 127) = 455 | (24, 633, 254) | **911** |
| `fp_is_inf`       | 639 | (13, 317, 127) = 457 | (26, 635, 254) | **915** |
| `fp_is_zero`      | 639 | ( 2, 317, 127) = 446 | ( 4, 635, 254) | **893** |
| `fp_is_subnormal` | 637 | ( 1, 316, 127) = 444 | ( 2, 633, 254) | **889** |

`_unc` is identical to forward at this mask, and the two are pinned separately
in `tests/goldens/fpfield.counts` — asserting `unc == forward` is the R6
mistake by name.

**SLOTS AND GATES COME APART BY ABOUT 30%**: 637 slots against 455 emitted
gates for `is_nan`. The difference is exactly the Phase-A slots whose control
is a constant `ZERO` lane — 53 fill lanes plus 53 zero constant lanes in the
exponent block, 12 plus 64 in the fraction block — and it is why the identity in
§3.1 is over slots.

**Where the X's come from, since three of the four counts look surprising.**
Each compute half's X's are `popcount(ce) + popcount(cf)` demotions plus one per
`lower_not1!`. `is_nan`: 11 + 1 = 12. `is_inf`: 11 + 2 = 13. `is_zero`: 0 + 2 = 2.
`is_subnormal`: 0 + 1 = 1. Nothing else in this module emits an X.

**There is NO upstream magnitude to compare against.** `BENCHMARKS.md` costs
whole `soft_*` routines, and a class predicate is three of `soft_fadd`'s ~40
lines; upstream also gives every constant a fresh 64-wire vector, so its ancilla
figure for the same three lines is not ours. PRD-v2's risk R14 already says these
magnitudes are not goldens.

| predicate | region | peak (region + `dst`) |
|---|---:|---:|
| `fp_is_nan`, `fp_is_subnormal` | 256 | 257 |
| `fp_is_inf`, `fp_is_zero`      | 257 | 258 |

## 5. Deltas from upstream

### 5.1 The views absorb BOTH the shift and the mask — the one real delta

K18's draft transcribes `ea = (a >> 52) & 0x7FF` as **wiring plus an `and`(64)
block** (its rows N1/N2), on D-K18-4's ground that a constant shift is wiring
and §7.2's ground that one block is owed per operator occurrence. **K22 makes
the mask wiring too**, and this is the module's one departure from a literal
reading of §7.2.

**The arithmetic, so the two readings can be compared rather than argued.** An
`and`(64) block over `(a_hi, K_0x7FF)` is 64 slots of `CCX(a_hi[i], K[i], r[i])`
into a fresh 64-bit span. Under the §3 fold table a `ZERO` constant control
elides the gate and a `ONE` demotes it, so at the all-quantum mask it emits 11
`CX` and costs **64 scratch qubits**; its output `r` is then a copy of exactly
the lanes the view names. The view gives the same 64-lane operand for **0 gates
and 0 qubits**, and every downstream use is a control, so no consumer can tell
the two apart except by cost.

**What is NOT given up.** The block still runs at `W = 64` over the assembled
view — §7.2's *"no narrowing towards the field width"* is untouched, and the
53 constant lanes stay in the operand where the fold table can see them. The
delta is about where an intermediate is MATERIALISED, not about its width.

**It is recorded rather than settled here.** PRD-v2 §7.2 is the source of
record and it says one block per operator occurrence; this module does not, and
K18's N2/N5 rows will disagree with M31 when M36 lands. **The reconciliation is
a document question and belongs in PRD-v2 §7.2 or a D-number, not in code.** If
§7.2 is read strictly, the fix is four `and`(64) blocks per predicate and
+256 scratch bits, and §3's identity gains `4 · C_and(64)`.

### 5.2 Constants are sources, not wires

`arith.jl:202` — *"Constants are always safe (their wires are freshly allocated
by resolve!)"* — is upstream's shape and PRD-v2 §7.3's refused alternative.
Ours cost nothing and fold. This is §7.3 applied, not a K22 decision.

### 5.3 `lower_not1!` rather than a negated control

Rule 4 admits only X/CX/CCX, so there is no negative-control gate to fold an
`==` polarity into. The alternative — emitting `X(raw); CCX(raw, other, flag);
X(raw)` and skipping the fresh wire — saves one bit and costs one slot, and is
**not adopted**: it is not in Bennett, Rule 1 forbids substituting it on our own
authority, and it makes the `and` row read a wire that two neighbouring slots
mutate.

### 5.4 One block type with a class field, not four

`fpclass.h` exports one `cq_fp_class_block` carrying a `cq_fp_class`, and four
separate Rule 7 kernel entry points with no class argument between them. Rule
7's *"no predicate parameter"* governs `cq_kernel_fn` — the KERNEL contract —
and is honoured. The block is K9's ten-predicates-one-body applied one layer
down, for K9's stated reason.

### 5.5 The classical short-circuit packs to a `uint64_t`

M16's `const_raw` is deliberately bit-serial because `icmp` ships at i80 and a
64-bit word would cap the kernel. No such cap exists here: PRD-v2 §1 scopes v2
to `f64`, the pattern IS 64 bits by definition, and §7.4 decides the
short-circuit is a C transcription of the Julia body over `uint64_t`. I5 is
untouched — the representation is still 64 tri-valued `cq_bit`s.

## 6. Confidence

### 6.1 Source-verified — read out of the pinned snapshot

- The four predicate spellings, at `fadd.jl:29`, `:31`, `:33` and `flog.jl:262`.
- The unpack, `fadd.jl:20-22`.
- `lower_eq!` `arith.jl:424-447`, `lower_not1!` `:474-478`, `lower_and!`
  `:268-272`.
- The seven constants, `softfloat_common.jl:8-14`.
- `arith.jl:202`'s constants-are-freshly-allocated note.

### 6.2 Measured by execution — Debug and Release, 2026-09-18

- §3.2's slot, gate and region table, and §4's peaks.
- `cq_eq_steps(64) = 317` and its `(0, 254, 63)` tuple, cross-checked against
  K09.md §7.3's own measured row.
- The four predicates against the HOST oracle over §7.12's fifteen singles, at
  the all-classical, all-quantum and exponent-lanes-only masks.
- The block driven standalone over caller scratch, palindromic at all three
  masks, with the region reclaimed against `cq_shadow_known_zero`.
- Two blocks at two offsets in one region, over 12 class pairs × 5 values.

### 6.3 Derived by hand, then confirmed by measurement

§3.2's whole table was derived before the kernel ran (11 demotions from
`popcount(0x7FF)`, 189 Phase-B gates per block, 52 vs 11 Phase-A copies) and
came out identical in all twelve cells. That is evidence the fold model in §2.3
is right, not merely that the kernel is self-consistent.

### 6.4 Highest-risk items, ranked

1. **§5.1's delta**, which contradicts K18's draft and a literal §7.2. It is a
   cost fork rather than a correctness fork — the two readings agree lane for
   lane — but the two modules must not ship disagreeing.
2. **The polarity of each `==`**, invisible to every structural instrument.
   Carried by L1 against the host and by
   `the_blocks_flag_is_the_predicate_not_its_negation`.
3. **The eleven-lane exponent view**, invisible to everything that does not
   evaluate a NEGATIVE operand. Carried by the two negative anchors and by the
   index-for-index view case.
4. **The slot boundaries**, carried by §2.3's scan and by nothing else.
5. **The scratch layout**, which §2.3's scan does NOT see — §2.3a is its own
   instrument and was written only after a mutant survived everything else.

### 6.5 Two DELIBERATELY equivalent mutants, recorded rather than left untested

Both measured green in both configurations, 2026-09-18, and both are genuinely
equivalent rather than uncovered. Recorded at the site, on the precedent of
`cq_rotate_rz_bit`'s `cq_angle_rz_row` collapse.

1. **Swapping the `and`'s two controls.** `CCX(x, y, t)` and `CCX(y, x, t)` are
   the same gate; nothing in this project distinguishes them, and nothing
   should. Not a coverage hole.
2. **Reading the `eq` flag as `orr[62]` instead of through `cq_eq_flag`.**
   `cq_eq_flag`'s branch is at `W == 1` and M31 instantiates `eq` only at 64, so
   the accessor and the rule return the same pointer here. K09.md §7.2 already
   states that this can be caught **nowhere but** `tests/test_kernel_cmp_block.inc`,
   whose fixture lays its region out as `orr ++ diff` on purpose. The call site
   keeps the accessor anyway: it is what makes a future consumer at `W == 1`
   correct, and weakening it to match the mutant is the move that finding
   forbids.
