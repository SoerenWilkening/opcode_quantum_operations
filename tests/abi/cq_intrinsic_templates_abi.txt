# cq_intrinsic_templates_abi.txt -- the llvm.* INTRINSIC half of the
# cq_template_* ABI, 389 declarations VERBATIM.
#
# WHAT THIS IS AND WHY IT IS NOT A REGENERABLE GOLDEN. It is an INDEPENDENT
# ORACLE, exactly as cq_templates_abi.txt is: the declarations CQ_lang's own
# tools/gen_templates.py produced from the SAME pinned yaml, by a different
# program that cannot share our reading of the naming rules. shim/gen_shim.py
# plus shim/gen_intrinsics.py expand intrinsic_table.yaml into names and
# signatures; a test that re-expanded them itself would share every
# transcription slip the generator made -- and this table brings THREE new
# shape spellings with it (`qql`, `qlq`, `qll`), a BARE `qqq` that is not
# spelled at all, two `_Float16`/`long double` operand types, and an `op` that
# appears TWICE at two arities. Diffing against THIS catches all of them.
# Nothing in libcqops may write this file.
#
# PROVENANCE, and the R3-shaped drift check that rides on it:
#   source        : CQ_lang runtime/cq_intrinsic_templates.h (generated)
#   CQ_lang HEAD  : c4dc68df935aa11d2d0496dbb8755855cda9f530
#   yaml-sha256   : b22cf3a3f4d0a731eb845923f3fabb82201c3224e9d8b61df18f4ee66104313e
#
# The sha above is the banner of that generated header AND the sha256 of our
# pinned third_party/cq_lang/intrinsic_table.yaml -- they are equal, which is
# what proves this manifest is the expansion of the yaml we actually ship.
# third_party/cq_lang/COMMIT.intrinsics records the pin; cmake/
# CqopsIntrinsicPin.cmake makes a drift in it a CONFIGURE-TIME hard error.
#
# To refresh: re-pin intrinsic_table.yaml first, then re-extract from
# CQ_lang's regenerated header. Never hand-edit a declaration.
